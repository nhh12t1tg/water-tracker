Privacy Policy

App: AquaWave: Water Tracker (com.nhh.aquawave)
Effective Date: November 23, 2026

AquaWave: Water Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. 100% Local Offline Storage for Health & Hydration Data

All personal hydration preferences and health metrics you enter in the app—including body weight, gender, activity level, daily water intake logs, streak counts, and reminder schedules—are stored exclusively on your device using Android local storage (SharedPreferences) with cloud backup disabled (allowBackup=false).

We do not operate external servers, require user accounts, or collect, transmit, or sell your personal health data.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob & UMP)

To keep the app free, AquaWave uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display advertisements and manage consent preferences (including GDPR/EEA compliance).

  • Google AdMob may collect and process your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and general diagnostic/interaction data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • You can review or update your personalized vs. non-personalized ad preferences at any time in the app's Settings → Privacy Preferences screen.

3. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET: Required only to load advertisements and consent forms via Google AdMob/UMP.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used solely to display hydration reminder alerts at your chosen intervals.
  • VIBRATE & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to provide haptic feedback and restore local reminder schedules after a device restart or timezone change.

We never request access to your location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, or external files.

4. Children's Privacy

AquaWave is intended for users aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
